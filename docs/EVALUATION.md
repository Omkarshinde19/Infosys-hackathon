# Evaluation results

Generated by the harness (300 paired scenarios × 7 policies, 60 s of simulated time each). Raw runs and the summary: `docs/evaluation.json`; charts on the dashboard's Evaluation page.

Policies: `no_heal` detects and diagnoses only. `reactive` diagnoses with rules and applies the first detour, with no twin test and no guardrails (an unsafe ablation). `guarded_reactive` uses rules and the first detour with the production safety checks. `native_shortest` reconverges shortest paths after three observed reachability ticks; it is a simplified routing reference, not a full OSPF or ECMP implementation. `twin` is NetHeal: rules + GNN diagnosis, twin-tested candidates behind the OPA guardrails. `predictive_twin` adds the forecaster and LSTM autoencoder. `rl` also adds the RL routing agent's proposal as one more candidate, tested like the rest (the live configuration).

In brackets: faults of that type that broke the SLA without healing. Every policy is judged on those same faults. Service scoring matches healing thresholds: every flow must be reachable, loss below 1%, latency at most 1.1 times its frozen healthy baseline. ## Reading these numbers

- The plant, twin, training generator and evaluation share the same queueing simulator and campus topology. These finite synthetic results establish no real-network accuracy or robustness to unseen topology, multiple faults or model mismatch.
- Pretrained model files were preserved. Their hashes identify what was loaded; their original training lineage was not reverified. Isolated oracle-target PPO scores are not substituted for operational results using the actual diagnosis.
- MTTD/MTTR medians include successes only; detection, diagnosis coverage, recovery counts and denominators expose failures. Native reconvergence can recover without a verified healer action and therefore has no healer MTTR.
- Collateral breach records service on flows outside the frozen original fault cohort after an action. This is observed association, not proof that the action caused it. Paired comparisons use the same incident seeds and fault specifications; random-number consumption may change when different routes change reachability.
- Recovery confidence intervals use Wilson binary-rate intervals. Paired deltas resample whole incidents, not correlated ticks/flows, with 2,000 deterministic bootstrap replicates. Intervals describe this scenario distribution and do not cover simulator bias. A single pair has no estimated bootstrap interval.

## Controlled unmodeled-delay stress

An independent synthetic observation perturbation adds 18% delay to `library-internet` and `lab_cs-internet` at 3–13 s and 30–40 s. The twin does not include this perturbation. 100 paired scenarios use the same fault seeds. Controls without an injected fault still receive the delay and are labeled disturbance controls, not healthy negatives; their alarms do not inflate a false-positive estimate. This bounded test exercises verification, rollback and relapse, and provides no evidence about emulator or real-network generalization.
